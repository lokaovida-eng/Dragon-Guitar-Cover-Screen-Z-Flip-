**Dragon Guitar** is an interactive guitar rhythm game custom-built for the outer cover screen of **Samsung Galaxy Z Flip** foldable smartphones. Play guitar riffs and hit rhythm notes directly on your cover screen without unfolding your device!

---

## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

- 🎸 **Cover Screen Native Gameplay**: Play rhythm guitar songs right on the outer Galaxy Z Flip display.
- ⚡ **Low-Latency Audio Engine**: Instant acoustic/electric guitar note response.
- 🐲 **Dragon Custom Artwork**: Custom high-resolution graphics optimized for outer display aspect ratios.
- 🔋 **Battery-Optimized**: Native Android canvas rendering for smooth performance and low battery consumption.
